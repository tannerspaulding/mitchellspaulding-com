File 04

How I make a game

  1. 1

    Dream it up

    I say the idea out loud: the worlds, the rules, what you're trying to find.

  2. 2

    Sketch and plan

    I figure out how it looks and works. My dad asks me lots of questions to make it better.

  3. 3

    Build it

    My dad and I build it together with AI, so my ideas turn into a real game fast.

  4. 4

    Play-test it

    I play it, find what's too easy or too hard, and we fix it. Then on to the next idea.
