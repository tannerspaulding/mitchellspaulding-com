File 01

How to play

  1. 1

    Look around

    Use the arrows to move from room to room. Tap doors, boxes and bushes to peek inside and behind things.

  2. 2

    Find the ducks

    Tap a duck when you spot it and it goes in your Duck Log. Easy ducks sit right out in the open. Legendary ones take real work.

  3. 3

    Help the helper

    Later worlds have a helper, like the Penguin or the Usher Owl. Every duck you find gives you a token. Hand them all over and the helper joins your log too.

  4. 4

    Unlock the next world

    Fill a world's Duck Log and the next world opens, from The Backyard all the way to The Spaceship.

Bonus: Pond Points

Play the mini games to win Pond Points, then spend them in Kingsley's shop on hats, new duck colors and special ducks.
