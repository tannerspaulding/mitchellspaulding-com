                        Ideas waiting for you

                        New duck and world ideas from players. Nobody else sees an idea until you approve it. Reject deletes it.

                          This month's vote

                          Approved ideas players can vote on. One vote per device per idea. Starts fresh on the 1st. Tap Built it once an idea is in the game: it leaves the vote, and the player who sent it sees "Your idea was built!" next time they open Quackers.

                            Built from player ideas

                            Ideas you've marked as built, newest first. Players see these in the Idea Box, with no names. Undo puts an idea back in the vote if it's still this month's.

                              Last month's winner

                              The idea with the most votes last month. Mark it as made once it's in the game, and players see that it happened.

                              Friends who joined

                              A friend counts when a new device opens a player's link and finds its first duck. Who invited whom isn't kept.

                              Leaderboard names

                              Most ducks first, newest first on a tie. Hide takes a name off the board right away; Show puts it back.

                                    Players are counted by a random code saved on their device, so one person on two devices counts as two, and clearing the browser starts a new player. IP addresses are never saved. Cities are kept only as a count per city per day, and play sessions aren't linked to players. Any player can delete their data from the game, and players who stop playing are deleted after 12 months.
